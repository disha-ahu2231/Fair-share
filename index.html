How it works

Simple as 1, 2, 3

FairShare keeps your group finances transparent, fair, and stress-free.

01
Create a Group
Start a group for your trip, apartment, or any shared context. Invite friends via a sharable link.
02
Add Expenses
Log expenses as they happen. Choose equal splits, custom amounts, or percentages.
03
Settle Up
FairShare calculates the minimum number of transactions to settle all debts. Pay and done.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is FairShare free to use? ›
Yes! FairShare's core features are completely free. A Pro plan adds analytics exports, priority support, and custom group themes.
How does the split calculation work? ›
FairShare uses a debt simplification algorithm to minimize the number of transactions needed. Instead of everyone paying everyone, it calculates the fewest transfers that settle all debts at once.
Can I use multiple currencies in one group? ›
Absolutely. You can add expenses in any supported currency. FairShare converts them to your group's base currency using live exchange rates.
Is my financial data secure? ›
Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use JWT-based authentication, never store payment credentials, and offer two-factor authentication.
How do I invite someone to a group? ›
Each group has a unique invite link you can share via WhatsApp, email, or any messaging app. Members can join without needing an account first.
